The power of restraint in visual storytelling

Minimal forms, bold materials, and deliberate whitespace often outlast a complicated layout. People remember the one frame that had room to breathe.

Restraint is not emptiness. It is choosing a material, a crop, and a sentence, then refusing the next three ideas that would dilute them.

When a story needs to be remembered, we design fewer scenes and give each one a job.
